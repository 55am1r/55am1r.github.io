Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · applies to the Expensy app for Android (package app.expensetracker)

In short
  • Expensy works on your phone. It has no server, and its maker never receives your data.
  • It reads payment messages from your bank and notifications from the payment apps you choose, to record your payments.
  • Your data stays in the app on your phone, in a backup folder you choose, and, if you connect it, in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared.

1. Who we are

Expensy is a free app made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at the address in section 11.

2. What Expensy reads, and why

WhatWhyWhere it stays
SMS messages (permissions to read and receive SMS)To find the payments your bank tells you about: money out, money in, the amount, the account and who it was with. Messages that aren't about money, like your personal texts, are ignored and never kept.On your phone

Sending messages

Expensy only sends a message when you tap send. A reminder by SMS is sent from your phone to the person you chose (permission to send SMS). A reminder by WhatsApp opens WhatsApp with the message ready; you send it yourself.

Other permissions

Expensy asks to post notifications (to tell you about new payments and reminders), to start after your phone restarts and to be left out of battery optimisation (so it doesn't miss payments), and for network access (used only for Google Drive, and Gmail if you connect it, as described below).

3. Google user data

Connecting Google is optional. Expensy asks Google only for the permissions below, and only when you turn on the feature that needs them.

Google Drive (backup)

If you connect Google Drive, Expensy uses the drive.appdata permission to save and read its backup file in a hidden app folder in your own Drive. It can't see, open or change any other file in your Drive. The backup goes directly from your phone to your Drive; we never see it.

Gmail (card statements, optional, in a future version)

If you choose to connect Gmail, Expensy will use read-only access to your mail (gmail.readonly) on your phone, only to find the credit card statement e-mails your card issuers send, download those statement PDFs, and read them on your phone to show your statements. It doesn't keep, change or send any other mail, and nothing from your mail leaves your phone.

Google profile picture (optional, in a future version)

If you choose "Use my Google picture", Expensy gets your Google account's profile picture to show it in the app.

Limited Use

Expensy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features above. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to anyone, not read by people, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

4. Where your data is stored

  • On your phone, in Expensy's private storage, which other apps can't read. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Expensy.
  • In a backup folder you choose on your phone (for example Documents/Expensy).
  • In your own Google Drive, in Expensy's hidden app folder, if you connect it.
  • In a future version, passwords for statement PDFs that you ask Expensy to remember are kept only on your phone, encrypted with your phone's security, and never put in backups.

A backup file holds your Expensy data. Anyone who can open your backup folder or your Google account could read it, so keep your phone and your Google account secure.

5. Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Expensy contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services. It has no server of its own; it connects to the internet only to reach Google Drive (and Gmail, if you connect it) for you.

6. Your choices

  • Turn off any permission in your phone's settings at any time. Expensy keeps working with what you allow; for example, without notification access it still reads bank SMS.
  • Remove Expensy's access to Google Drive (or Gmail) at any time in your Google Account, under Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services.

7. Deleting your data

  • On your phone: uninstalling Expensy removes its data from your phone. Delete any backup files in the folder you chose.
  • In Google Drive: open Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → Expensy → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • A future version adds Settings → Delete account, which erases everything Expensy holds, on your phone and in your Drive, in one step.

8. Keeping it safe

Expensy can ask for your fingerprint, face or phone PIN when it opens. Your data is processed on your phone and only travels to the places you choose in section 4.

9. Children

Expensy isn't meant for children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect anyone's data; all data stays with the person using the app.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: expensy-support@googlegroups.com

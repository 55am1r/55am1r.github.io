Every rupee, caught the moment it moves.

Expensy is an Android app that reads the payment messages your bank and your payment apps already send you, right on your phone, and turns them into a clear picture of where your money goes, who owes you, and what your cards want this month.

FreeNo adsNo sign-upWorks on your phone, not on a serverYour data stays yours

Expensy is in testing and isn't on Google Play yet.

What it does

Catches your payments

Reads bank SMS and notifications from the payment apps you choose (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED, your bank's app) and records each payment, with the amount, the account and who it went to.

Shows where it went

A home screen with your spending and money in, by day and by category, and every payment in one list you can search.

Splits and paybacks

Tag a payment as yours, for someone else, or split. Expensy keeps each person's balance and notices when they pay you back.

Friendly reminders

Once a month it helps you remind the people who owe you, by WhatsApp or SMS, in your own words, only when you tap send.

Card statements coming next

Each credit card's statement, minimum and due date from the messages the card sends, with a reminder before it's due.

Backed up, privately

A backup in a folder on your phone and, if you connect it, in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only Expensy can see.

Why it was made

“I never really knew where my money went. A tea here, a cab there, a dinner I paid for and forgot to ask back, and the month was gone. So I made Expensy to catch every rupee the moment it moves. Now I know where it goes, and I keep more of it.”

From the maker of Expensy

Your privacy, in short

On your phone

Messages are read and understood on your phone. Expensy has no server, and nothing is sent to its maker.

Only what it needs

Expensy looks for money messages from banks and payment apps. Your personal texts are ignored and never kept.

Your own backup

Backups go only to places you choose: your phone, and your own Google Drive.
